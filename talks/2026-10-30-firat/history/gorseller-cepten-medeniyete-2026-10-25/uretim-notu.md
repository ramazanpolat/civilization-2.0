# Açılış görseli — Karttan telefona

- Araç: built-in `image_gen`.
- Kaynak: `karttan-telefona.png`.
- Sunum varlığı: `karttan-telefona.webp`.
- Kullanım: Cepten Medeniyete v5 açılışı. Kavramsal stüdyo görseli; aile arşivi veya tarihî belge olarak sunulmaz.
- WebP sürümü yalnızca dosya boyutu için sıkıştırıldı; kompozisyon değişmedi.

## Kullanılan prompt

```text
Use case: productivity-visual. Asset type: premium editorial cover photograph for a Turkish university talk about how everyday communication technology changes shared life, relationships and social norms. Wide landscape 16:9, refined realistic studio still-life, not a slide and absolutely no designed typography. Warm ivory paper tabletop fills background (#f5f1e8). On the right two thirds, arrange a slightly aged cream greeting postcard, a pale paper envelope and an elegant unbranded dark smartphone. Postcard shows a small muted oxblood botanical illustration and faint indistinct handwritten ink strokes, with NO readable words or names. Smartphone display is dark and quiet with just two simple cream message bubble outlines and no text, no logo, no brand UI. A thin burgundy thread curls gracefully from the postcard to the phone, suggesting a continuous human connection through changing media. Warm side sunlight, tactile paper grain, soft museum-like shadows, sophisticated magazine photography, understated burgundy and brass accents, beautifully composed negative space. Leave the entire left 40 percent mostly empty warm ivory for large title typesetting in the HTML; keep all physical objects and thread in the right 60 percent, no objects crossing the left quarter. Objects fully visible, no humans, no hands, no robots, no neon, no futuristic holograms, no numbers, no watermark. The emotional idea is continuity of relationships from handwritten greetings to instant messages, not workplace productivity.
```
